YuGiOh Master Duel Banlist - September 2026

September 8th, 2026 via Wargamer

The Yugioh Master Duel banlist is an essential tool for anyone who wants to duel online, but it's not always easy to track. With digital Yugioh expanding all the time, it needs its own (quite frequent) card bans to fix what gets broken. We keep you up to date with any and all changes in this guide, so you know the latest bans with minimal fuss.

For the physical TCG, be sure to bookmark our Yugioh banlist guide, too. Or, for more serious collectors, check out the most expensive YuGiOh cards money can buy.

What is the YuGiOh Master Duel banlist?

The Master Duel card pool doesn't look quite the same as the one from the physical trading card game, so a separate banlist is needed to keep things balanced. Despite this, the Master Duel banlist follows roughly the same rules as the regular YuGiOh banlist.

By this, we mean there are three degrees of 'ban' a card can be subject to:

  • Forbidden - Can't be used in the Main Deck, Extra Deck, or Side Deck
  • Limited, Limited 1 - Can only have one copy in the Main Deck, Extra Deck, and Side Deck
  • Semi-limited, Limited 2 - Can have two copies max in the Main Deck, Extra Deck, and Side Deck combined

If a card is not on the banlist at all, it's referred to as Unlimited.

And that's it for the Master Duel banlist as it stands! For more on trading card games, here's all you need to know about the MTG banlist.
