Necromunday: The September 2026 FAQ & Errata Hot Take

September 7th, 2026 via Goonhammer
We're a little over a month from the release of Necromunda Skirmish and Games Workshop has already dropped a FAQ. This covers both the core book and both of the Gang books. Here's what popped out for us.

Necromunda Skirmish: Core Rulebook

The errata to the core rulebook doesn't bring about any significant changes, but instead focuses on aligning the wording of the rules with the intent. Many of these changes were likely things players were already doing, such as charging a Seriously Injured Fighter.

  • Mounted units can only move forward when using a Charge or Dash action, as otherwise the limitation on a single 90° turn is meaningless.
  • The Charge and Engage actions now include the ability to be within 1" of a Seriously Injured model, as previously there was no way to actually use the free Coup de Grâce option.
  • The interaction between Ammo (X+) and Rapid Fire (X) is clarified so that a failed Ammo (X+) test does count as rolling an additional ammo symbol.
  • The Quickening power was corrected to a Simple action; apparently the copy editor missed that Basic actions no longer exist. Oops.
  • Drop Rigs and Grapnels have been adjusted so that mounted fighters can no longer use them. Van Saar grav cutters win again.

The FAQs provide some helpful clarifications, particularly with respect to the Suppressed and Seriously Injured state and the impact of Out of Ammo on weapons with multiple profiles. The clarification on Wyrd Powers is also very useful; they're acquired like skills but aren't skills with respect to being Injured or rolling a 12 for Advancement.

  • If you blow up a Transport, and the Fighters inside can't be placed and are thus taken Out of Action, then the attacking model that blew up the Transport gains XP for the Fighters as well.
  • Falling when shot works as if the model was forcibly moved off the edge by being put into the Suppressed or Seriously Injured state.
  • Games Workshop clarified how the effects of Suppressed and Seriously Injured affect what a model can do. Specifically, a model's state at the beginning of their activation determines how many actions they have and if they can perform a Recovery test. If a model obtains the Suppressed or Seriously Injured state during their activation then they won't lose the state (or make a Recovery roll) at the end.
  • The Suppressed state was clarified to only occur as part of resolving a ranged attack.
  • The Treat Ally action also has the Fighter count as granting assistance to the Figher who is making the Recovery test.
  • Heavy weapons were addressed. A Heavy weapon that somehow gets the Assault trait cannot be fired after a Dash action. If it has the Independent trait then it can be fired as part of an Aimed Shot or Shoot action. The difference is that Dash specifically provides a free Shoot action (which cannot be used to fire a heavy weapon), while the Independent trait allows the weapon to be fired whenever the model performs a ranged attack (and is not a specific action).
  • Out of Ammo was clarified. If a weapon with multiple profiles experiences a jam, only the profile used will jam. So combat shotguns suck a little less than we thought. The Reliable trait will cancel Out of Ammo results for failed Ammo (X+) tests. Fighters still have to roll tests if a weapon with the Ammo (X+) trait is used in close combat.
  • Wyrd Powers are... weird. While they're treated as skills that can be purchased as part of the Primary Skill Set, they are themselves not skills and are unaffected by the Injured condition. This also means that a player who rolls a 12 for Advancement cannot suddenly become a psyker.
  • A roll of 12 for Advancement is insanely cool. You can access any skill, including gang-specific skills or Inherent skills like Juggernaut.
  • Fighters or Trazior sentry guns were confirmed to be allowed to be deployed on Vehicles already deployed in the deployment zone. A sentry gun placed in a location other than the transport area of a Vehicle will fall off when the Vehicle moves.
  • Equipment was clarified. Equipment from a model that dies from any way (including being captured) goes into the gang's Stash, and a model may not move equipment into a Stash during the same Post-cycle Sequence that they purchased it.
  • Weapons with multiple profiles can use various skills (such as Precision Shot) so long as the particular profile meets the criteria of that skill.
  • A Mount sets the Movement characteristic to a particular value, and effects that modify that value (such as a stimm-slug stash) don't work. Presumably this would also work on Injuries like Hobbled.
Still trying to figure out how this is a Mount, but there you go. Credit: Fowler

Gangs of the Underhive

Lots of changes here. Several gangs got missing items added, and a desperately needed adjustment was made to the Redemptionist champion so that it was more in alignment with their Pious brethren. The Van Saar Arachni-Rig received a necessary correction (a Vehicle cannot be a Flyer so something had to change), but it has left the model in a very awkward state.

  • Cawdor got some tweaks. Redemptionist Deacons got cheaper! 130 credits was absurd. 100 credits is far better. Also stun lances don't exist! It was supposed to be shock lances.
  • If your sneaky Delaque player was trying to convince you that they could take an Escher Cutter, Goliath Mauler, or Outrider Quad because they didn't say Exclusive... well now they do.
  • Orlock players can once again immolate their own guys with portable stars; the plasma pistol was added to the House Orlock Equipment List. Also, the Jump Booster, which modifies Movement and would therefore not work with a Mount, is now prohibited from being equipped on a Fighter with a Mount.
  • Vaan Saar Arachni-rigs got a bunch of tweaks. They're no longer a Flying subtype, but instead gained Skimmer, Hybrid, and Loner. GW had two options; either they could have made it a Brute instead of a Vehicle or addressed the Flying subtype. They chose the latter. This does pose some problems, as a Vehicle cannot climb up or down a vertical surface and the Skimmer type doesn't grant any benefits against falling. The Arachni-rig did get back the flamer and harpoon launcher options, so the obvious solution is to fit the Arachni-rig with every weapon and have fun horrifying people in Zone Mortalis.
  • Enforcers didn't have the ability to take Magnacles. Now they can; they're 20 credits.
  • The Palanite Justicar Delegation was adjusted so that Magistrates don't have the ability to redirect hits to Seriously Injured Bailiffs; they have to be Active or Suppressed.
  • Venator gangs cannot purchase things from the Trading Post with a TP of E unless it appears on the model's Equipment List.
  • Corpse Grinder Cult Initiates only get one chance to be promoted to a Ganger when they reach 13 XP.
Joe's (JoJo_Baggins) Squats at the GHO 2026 Necromunda Weekender. Credit: Kevin Genson

Gangs of the Outlands

The biggest change here is GW addressing obviously broken Extra Arm ability provided to Genestealer Cultists. As written the arm completely disregarded the downside of a Heavy weapon, allowing the Fighter to move and shoot like a regular gun. That has since been edited so that everyone is equally hampered by Heavy weapons. Squats also got a bit of a boost with their seismic grenades getting more range.

  • The Extra Arm ability explicitly prohibits Fighters from performing a Shoot or Move action during the same activation they perform a Braced Shot action. This addresses a pretty major issue people had with GSC, where a third arm made them better than anyone else in the game at carrying Heavy weapons.
  • Ironhead seismic grenades now have a range of 24" instead of 12" which makes them pretty damn good.
  • Corrupted and Clan House Outcast gangs explicitly do not receive access to special rules, Skill Sets, or Territory Boons available to the original gang. They do count as the original gang for purposes of the Hatred condition.
  • An Outcast Leader of the Wyrd Archetype does grant the Outcast Hive Scum the Wyrd Subtype, but they do not gain a free Wyrd Power.
  • When dealing with mixed gangs, such as adding a Chaos Helot Cult Witch to a Chaos Corrupted Escher gang, models using whatever Equipment List is listed in their entry.
If you get rid of the hero girder the Arachni-rig defaults to looking like it's about to fall on its face. Now we know why. Credit: 40khamslam.

Wrapping Up

Bitter Van Saar players aside (and let's be honest we deserve it) the changes here are pretty solid. The errata mostly addresses missing pieces and culls potential issues like Extra Arms breaking how Heavy weapons work. The FAQs are also useful, particularly with respect to Wyrd powers, the difference between starting an activation with a state and receiving it during the action, and how awesome it is to roll as 12 on the Advancement table. It should be interesting to see how the Gang Journals mix things up in the next few months.
